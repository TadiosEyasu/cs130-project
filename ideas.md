  ## How I Plan to Answer My Question

**Question: Which industries have the most billionaires?**

I would use Python to go through the billionaires dataset and count how many billionaires are in each industry. I would use a loop to look at each billionaire and keep track of how many are in each industry. Then I would compare the totals to find out which industry has the most billionaires.
